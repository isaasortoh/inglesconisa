Week 13 Mini

Speak Up
in Meetings.

Practice three skills that help you participate naturally: building on an idea, buying yourself time to think, and asking for clarification.

Skill 1 — Build on an idea

Building on that…

Use this when you want to connect directly to what someone else just said and add your own perspective.

“Building on that…”
“Building on that, I think the timeline is another factor we should consider.”
Skill 2 — Buy yourself a moment

That’s a good question.

This is a natural phrase that can give you a moment to organize your thoughts. You do not have to answer immediately to sound confident. 

“That’s a good question. My initial thought is....”
Professional, calm, and conversational — without sounding unprepared.
Skill 3 — Clarify naturally

Just to make sure I’m following…

Use this when you understood most of the conversation but want to confirm one important detail.

“Just want to make sure I’m following…”
“Just want to make sure I’m following, who’s taking the next step on this?”
