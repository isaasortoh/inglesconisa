Week 14 Mini

Say Less.
Sound Better.

Make workplace English shorter, clearer, and more natural — without losing professionalism.

Skill 1 — Get to the point

Lead with the update.

Instead of:
“I wanted to provide you with an update regarding the project.”
Try:
“Quick update on the project…”
Lead with what they actually need to know.
Skill 2 — Sound natural

Correct doesn’t always mean natural.

Formal:
“I will verify and communicate with you afterward.”
Natural:
“I’ll double-check and get back to you.”
Use the shortest phrase that still sounds clear and professional.
Skill 3 — Give the next step

Problem + next step.

Defensive:
“I cannot finish because Accounting has not sent the information.”
Stronger:
“I’m waiting on Accounting, so I’ll finish it as soon as I have the numbers.”
Explain the situation, then tell them what happens next.
